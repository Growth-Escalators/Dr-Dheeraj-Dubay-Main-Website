## Rules

1. Do not create a second Jaipur landing page for an intent that already has an owner above.
2. Supporting condition/blog/Hindi pages should use descriptive internal anchors pointing to the owner URL.
3. “Best” queries may be targeted contextually (for example, “how to choose the best…”), but the site should not make unsupported comparative claims that Dr. Dubay is objectively the best.
4. Educational procedure pages may explain techniques, risks and recovery; commercial cost pages own price/insurance intent.
5. City pages outside Jaipur should target travel/service-area intent and must not compete with the Jaipur owner pages.
